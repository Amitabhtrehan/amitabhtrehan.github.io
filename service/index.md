# Academic service

I founded and head Durham Computer Science's NESTiD research group and contribute to the distributed computing and theoretical computer science communities through conference organisation, programme committees, reviewing and doctoral assessment.

## Selected roles

- General Chair, SIROCCO 2026, Durham.
- Workshops Chair and Conference Committee member, ICDCN 2023 and 2024.
- Programme Committee member for conferences including PODC, ICDCN, ICDCS, SSS and I-SPAN.
- Chief Organiser, 32nd British Colloquium of Theoretical Computer Science, 2016.
- Workshop Co-Chair, TOPIC@PODC 2018.
- Fellow of the UK Higher Education Academy.
